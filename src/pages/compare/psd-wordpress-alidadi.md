**A comparison of a final WordPress theme to its original PSD mockup for Dr. Alidadi's website, by Joe Rhoney.**

This was a project for a client named Dr. Alidadi in 2017. I followed the PSD mockup provided by the design team. There were subtle changes in spacing and font sizes decided upon during the development process, some of which were made by the Production Manager, some were suggested by me based upon my design experience. Aside from that, I kept the layout as close to the PSD as possible, avoiding any unnecessary deviations.
