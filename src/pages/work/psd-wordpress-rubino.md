**A comparison of a final WordPress theme to its original PSD mockup for Dr. Rubino's website, by Joe Rhoney.**

At Gilleard Marketing, in 2017, I worked on a project for a client named Dr. Rubino. Following the PSD mockup provided by the design team, I maintained the layout, colors, fonts and over all look. The content had not yet been completed at this point. A few subtle design changes and images were determined by the Production Manager after the design phase. Once the content was complete, I was able to finish building out the webite.
