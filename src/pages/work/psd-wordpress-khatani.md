**A comparison of a final WordPress theme to its original PSD mockup for Dr. Khatani's website, by Joe Rhoney.**

I built this website at Gilleard Marketing for a client named Dr. Khatani in 2017. I followed the PSD mockup provided by the designer, accept for a few changes in the colors, fonts, and photos, which were decided upon by the Production Manager. I contributed a few design suggestions myself, which were approved and appreciated as well.

Note: some photos have been replaced to avoid copyright issues.
