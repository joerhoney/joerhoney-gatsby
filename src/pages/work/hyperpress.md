**Landing page for a high-performance WordPress hosting service, by Joe Rhoney.**

A WordPress theme with an animated starfield background and a fireball effect, for a unique and memorable flare. The animation is optimized with WebGPU for high performance, with infinite randomized generation and no looping.

The page layout is high-tech and responsive, built on a clean, repeatable design system that the client can expand upon without breaking the theme.
